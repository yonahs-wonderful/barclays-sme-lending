Suggested run-through:
1. Open Talia and start a fresh session.
2. Ask: "I've just signed a new contract with a major automotive manufacturer and need a £300,000 increase to my credit facility to fund it."
3. Choose RCF amendment + FX hedge and click Confirm & continue.
4. Upload the two PDFs in this package. Talia explains the £780,000 pipeline versus £760,000 signed-contract variance.
5. Select the business current account ending 2910 and click Use this account once to submit.
6. Open the new CoreBank Lending Entry task in USA / Barclays Activities to watch the computer agent. Wait for its task status to show Completed before describing the entry as finished.

Use the architecture and cockpit as companion views to explain the wider agent story and human review. Their presence alone is not evidence that a live case or human approval has completed. Wonderful links require access to the USA / Barclays workspace.

The conversation and computer-use automation are live; customer data, document checks and credit-review results are demo fixtures. No real credit approval or funds movement occurs. If the timed chat confirmation appears, it is explicitly simulated, not a verification of the computer task.
